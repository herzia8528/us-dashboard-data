Airport coordinates and identifiers: OurAirports (https://ourairports.com/data/), public domain.
Bundled US-only extract obtained from the DataHub airport-codes mirror:
https://github.com/datasets/airport-codes
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/datasets/airport-codes/main/archive/data.csv

Rebuild with tools/map-data/make_airport_file.py. Closed facilities and regions outside the
50 states plus DC are omitted. ICAO/GPS, IATA, FAA/local, and dataset identifiers are retained.
The airport catalog is an offline geographic lookup, independent of the FAA live feed.
